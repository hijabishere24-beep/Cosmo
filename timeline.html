Space Exploration Milestones

A complete timeline of humanity's journey into the cosmos.

1950s – 1970s // Dawn of Spaceflight & Moon Landing

OCT 1957

Sputnik 1 Launched

The Soviet Union launches the world's first artificial satellite, transmitting a simple radio signal for 21 days and initiating the Space Race.

NOV 1957

First Living Creature in Orbit

Laika, a stray dog from Moscow, becomes the first living organism to orbit Earth onboard the Soviet spacecraft Sputnik 2.

APR 1961

First Human in Space

Cosmonaut Yuri Gagarin orbits Earth aboard Vostok 1 during a 108-minute flight, becoming the first human to fly into outer space.

MAR 1965

First Spacewalk (EVA)

Soviet cosmonaut Alexei Leonov conducts the first Extravehicular Activity, tethered outside his Voskhod 2 capsule for 12 minutes.

JUL 1969

Apollo 11 Moon Landing

NASA's Apollo 11 mission lands on the Moon. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin become the first humans to walk on the lunar surface.

DEC 1972

Apollo 17 (Final Crewed Moon Mission)

Astronauts Eugene Cernan and Harrison Schmitt spend over 3 days on the lunar surface, marking humanity's last crewed voyage to the Moon of the 20th century.

SEP 1977

Voyager 1 & 2 Launched

NASA launches the Voyager probes to explore the outer gas giants. Voyager 1 would eventually become the first human-made object to reach interstellar space in 2012.

1980s – 2000s // Space Stations, Shuttles & Mars Exploration

APR 1981

First Space Shuttle Flight (STS-1)

NASA launches Space Shuttle Columbia, opening an era of reusable winged spacecraft designed for low-Earth orbit missions.

FEB 1986

Mir Space Station Assembly Begins

The Soviet Union launches the core module of Mir, establishing the first modular orbital space station continuously inhabited for 12 years.

APR 1990

Hubble Space Telescope Deployed

Space Shuttle Discovery places the Hubble Space Telescope into orbit, revolutionizing astronomy with crystal-clear images of galaxies and nebulae.

JUL 1997

Mars Pathfinder & Sojourner Rover

NASA lands Pathfinder on Mars, releasing Sojourner—the first wheeled rover to travel on another planet's surface.

NOV 1998

International Space Station (ISS) Construction

The Zarya module is launched into orbit, beginning a global collaboration between NASA, Roscosmos, ESA, JAXA, and CSA to build the ISS.

JAN 2004

Spirit & Opportunity Land on Mars

NASA's twin rovers land on opposite sides of Mars, finding overwhelming geological evidence that liquid water once existed on the Martian surface.

2010s – Present // Commercial Space, Black Holes & JWST

DEC 2015

First Orbital Rocket Booster Landing

SpaceX successfully lands a Falcon 9 first-stage booster back on Earth, proving commercial orbital rocket reusability is possible.

APR 2019

First Direct Image of a Black Hole

The Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) collaboration captures the first-ever direct visual image of a supermassive black hole at the center of galaxy M87.

FEB 2021

Perseverance Rover & Ingenuity Helicopter

Perseverance lands in Jezero Crater to search for ancient microbial life, carrying Ingenuity—the first aircraft to achieve powered flight on another planet.

DEC 2021

James Webb Space Telescope (JWST) Launch

The most powerful space telescope ever built launches to Lagrange Point 2 (L2) to observe the earliest galaxies formed after the Big Bang.

NOV 2022

NASA Artemis I Mission

An uncrewed Orion capsule circles the Moon and returns safely, laying the groundwork for returning humans to the lunar surface under the Artemis Program.
